The app is a tabbed dashboard with a persistent strip: Conversation (LLM
output highlighted as it is spoken, interim transcriptions, tool calls,
interruptions), Latency (per-turn breakdown bars, p50 and p95, speaking
timeline), Workers (tree, jobs, bus feed), Metrics (selectable series,
summary table, startup timing) and Logs (level filter, search, pinned
errors). The standalone app reconnects with backoff and can record and
replay sessions. Python 3.11 and Pipecat 1.3 are now the minimums.
