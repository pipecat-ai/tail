Tail is rebuilt on Pipecat 1.x. `TailObserver` now runs Pipecat's latency,
function call, service metrics, error, speech, turn tracking and startup
timing observers alongside RTVI, and forwards structured log records.
`TailServer` is a worker on a `WorkerRunner` that reports workers, jobs and
bus traffic. `TailRunner` is rebuilt on `WorkerRunner`. A setup file attaches
Tail to any bot through `PIPECAT_SETUP_FILES`.
